# ACC Portal

Welcome to the official repository for the **ACC Portal** — the online home of **Wells Arts & Comic Club (ACC)**.

ACC is a student creative club for people who enjoy comics, drawing, writing, character design, world-building, storytelling, planning, collaboration, and making strange ideas into something real.

The ACC Portal is meant to be a cozy, easy-to-use place where members can find club resources, creative prompts, music, projects, and information about what is happening in the club.

You do **not** need to be “good at drawing” to belong in ACC. Comics need artists, writers, planners, designers, world builders, dialogue people, character creators, editors, and people who simply have cool ideas.

---

## What Is ACC?

**Wells Arts & Comic Club**, or **ACC**, is a positive and encouraging place to explore creativity without pressure to be perfect.

The club focuses on making original stories and comics in many different ways. Members can draw, write, create characters, build worlds, plan plots, make storyboards, design costumes, work on dialogue, give feedback, or collaborate with other members.

ACC is built around a few simple ideas:

- Everyone starts somewhere.
- Rough drafts count.
- Weird ideas are welcome.
- Sharing is optional.
- Helpful feedback makes projects better.
- Every creative role matters.
- Original characters and stories deserve a place to grow.

---

## About the Portal

The ACC Portal is a student-built website made to support the club outside of meetings.

Depending on the current version, the portal may include:

- Club announcements and meeting information
- Embedded club slides, documents, or planning pages
- Comic and art activity prompts
- Character-creation tools
- Music links or playlists for drawing and writing
- Creative project links
- Resources for comics, storytelling, and art
- Information about ACC’s original characters and stories
- Interactive pages, hidden details, or small character interactions

The goal is not to make the website feel like a school assignment. It should feel like a creative clubhouse: organized enough to be useful, but welcoming enough that members want to come back.
